P.06 — Method

Built to be owned, not rented.

Offline by construction, not as a marketing line. There is no account to lose access to and no server that can go dark on you.

Clinical nutrition, not calorie guessing

Thirteen vitamins, nine minerals, omega ratios and fibre, matched against your own profile — with a food table verified against USDA and ICMR data and stored entirely offline.

Your data never leaves your device

Built on Dexie over IndexedDB. No accounts, no analytics, no telemetry. Weight logs, photos and notes stay in local, sandboxed storage — and export in full whenever you ask.

Native by construction

React, Vite and Capacitor compiled for Android, with timers held by a foreground service so nothing drifts during sleep or Doze mode.

Made for the ledger, not the feed

No streaks engineered to guilt you back in. Levels and badges exist to mark real consistency, and the monthly dossier reads it back to you plainly.
