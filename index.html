Clinical-grade nutrition, not just calorie counting

Most fitness apps stop at calories and macros. Lock In tracks 43 bioactives — 13 vitamins, 9 minerals, omega-3/6 ratios, EPA, DHA, fiber, and sodium — with personalized RDA targets based on your biological profile.

  • USDA and ICMR verified food database works entirely offline
  • Category adequacy scores detect chronic micronutrient gaps
  • Quick-add frequently logged items for sub-3-second daily entries

Your data stays on your device. Period.

Lock In uses Dexie.js over IndexedDB — everything lives in your hardware's local database. No accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics, no telemetry. Your weight logs, photos, and notes never touch the cloud.

  • Progress photos stored in sandboxed local storage, not a cloud bucket
  • Stealth private vault with triple-tap activation & PIN keypad
  • Full data export so you always maintain complete data ownership

Real native engineering, not a web wrapper

Built with React 19, Vite 5, and Capacitor 8 for native Android performance. Focus timers use an Android Foreground Service to eliminate timer drift during Doze mode and screen sleep.

  • Zero timer drift during Doze mode and lock screen states
  • Client-side PDF generation with jsPDF — zero server round-trips
  • Hardware camera integration with canvas photo compression

Designed for daily discipline, not engagement tricks

Three visual themes (Light, Dark, Ultra-Dark) with 13 accent colors. Haptic feedback on check-ins. Drag-to-reorder tasks. Gamification with XP, levels, and milestone badges that reward consistency.

  • Executive PDF dossiers available in both light and dark themes
  • Weekly insights synthesize all 6 domains into a single DRI score
  • Bidirectional note-to-task sync with automatic task cleanup
